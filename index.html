This works by playing the file out loud through your speakers while your microphone listens, using the same speech recognition as live dictation — there's no way around that without a separate server-side transcription service. Turn your volume up, keep the room quiet, and don't wear headphones (the mic needs to hear the playback). It takes as long as the recording runs — including multi-hour files — and adds onto whatever's already in the transcript below. The button above will show progress as elapsed / total time. This tries to keep your screen awake automatically for long files, but for anything over an hour it's still worth plugging the device in and not manually locking the screen, since a sleeping device will stop the mic mid-file.
